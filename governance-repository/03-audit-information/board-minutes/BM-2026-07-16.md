## 2. DSP-001: manual engraving transcription until full rollout

The Ops Lead (requester) explained the problem. The Engraving Queue full rollout falls after the Q4 freeze, so production between T1 (2026-09-14) and the rollout would still be manual. The text is captured in a structured form from T1, so operatives can **copy and paste** it from the Shopify order page instead of retyping it.
- The Ops Lead did not vote. Quorum: MD + Finance Manager.
- **Decision:** granted until 2027-01-31, with mitigations: second-person check on every engraved order, and a weekly error-rate report.
- Recorded in the AR [dispensations register](../../../architecture-repository/05-governance-log/dispensations.md) as DSP-001. Action **GA-004** created (weekly report).

## 3. DSP-002: engraving PC local Windows account

The laser vendor's software does not run under a managed/SSO Windows profile, which conflicts with STD-05 / REQ-12.
- The Ops Lead did not vote. Quorum: MD + Finance Manager.
- **Decision:** granted until 2027-07-16, with a review at 6 months. Mitigations: VLAN isolation (STD-09), no email or browsing, and a strong unique password in 1Password.
- Action **GA-005** created: ask the vendor for SSO support.
- The Finance Manager asked why 12 months rather than 6. The answer: the cause is outside our control, and the vendor's next major release is not expected before spring 2027. The Board asked for this reasoning to be written into a dispensation policy ([POL-03](../../01-reference-data/policies/POL-03-dispensation-policy.md), approved on 2026-08-13).
